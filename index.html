Bolt

Bolt RL Environments

Bolt is a GPU-accelerated musculoskeletal simulator with native support for OpenSim models, designed for massively parallel environments at hundreds to thousands of times real-time speed (in aggregate) on a single GPU.

Massively parallel

Thousands of environments simulated at once on one GPU.

OpenSim support

Natively supports many existing OpenSim models without modification.

RL-ready

Pre-built RL integrations are available. Policies train in under a day on a single GPU.

Demos

The following motions were generated without any motion demonstrations, using only a simple reward function: maximize speed without hyperextending joints. Diverse behavior is obtained via task-specific episode termination conditions.

Technical paper
