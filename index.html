Bolt

Bolt is a GPU-accelerated simulator for OpenSim models, designed for reinforcement learning-based predictive simulation at scale.

Bolt RL Environments

Overview

Consistent with OpenSim

Bolt natively supports OpenSim models without modification.

  • Implements the same physics algorithms as Simbody
  • Simulates the OpenSim muscle dynamics (including elastic tendon)
  • Supports force-based contacts and constraints with error-controlled integration

High throughput

Bolt enables massively parallel environments at hundreds-to-thousands of times real-time speed.

  • Thousands of environments simulated in parallel on one GPU
  • Reinforcement learning policies train in under a day

Demos

The following motions were generated without any motion demonstrations, using only a simple reward function: maximize speed without hyperextending joints. Diverse behavior is obtained via task-specific episode termination conditions.
